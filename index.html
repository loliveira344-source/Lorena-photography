About

Brazilian photographer Lorena Oliveira is drawn to the raw beauty of the natural world and the unscripted, intimate moments that flash by in an instant. She feels a scene before she frames it- an intuitive, perceptive approach that brings depth and authenticity to every photo.

A woman of the world, she's lived across South America, the US, and Europe, and traveled to over 30 countries. Fluent in English, Spanish, and Portuguese, she brings intention, cultural depth, and a focus on human connection to every partnership. She is based in London.
